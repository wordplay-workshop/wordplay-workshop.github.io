## Frequently Asked Questions

### Submissions

**Q: Can I submit work that has been published elsewhere?**
A: Yes! We welcome papers that have been published or submitted to other venues. However, you must acknowledge the original publication in your submission.

**Q: What's the difference between the main track and CPDC track?**
A: The main track is for general research papers (4-8 pages) and extended abstracts (2 pages). The CPDC track is specifically for technical reports from participants in the Common-sense Persona-Grounded Dialogue Challenge competition.

**Q: Are the proceedings archival?**
A: No, the workshop proceedings are non-archival. Accepted papers will be posted on the workshop website but will not appear in the EMNLP proceedings.

### Presentations

**Q: Will all accepted papers be presented?**
A: Yes! All accepted papers will be presented as posters. Additionally, the program committee will select a few papers for oral presentations.

**Q: Do I need to attend in person?**
A: The workshop will be held in Suzhou, China. Please check the main EMNLP 2025 website for information about remote participation options.

### Competition

**Q: Do I need to participate in the CPDC competition to submit to the workshop?**
A: No! The competition is optional. You can submit to the main track regardless of competition participation.

**Q: Can I submit both a main track paper and a CPDC technical report?**
A: Yes, if you're participating in the competition and have other research to share.

### Contact

**Q: Who should I contact if I have more questions?**
A: Please reach out to us at [wordplay.workshop.organizers@gmail.com](mailto:wordplay.workshop.organizers@gmail.com)
